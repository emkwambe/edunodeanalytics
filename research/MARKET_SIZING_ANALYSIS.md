- **Software portion:** <10% of total technology budget
- **Total US school tech spending:** $30 billion annually
- **Charter-specific challenges:** Reviewing software contracts under conservative enrollment assumptions post-ESSER

## 6. Key Market Insights

### 6.1 Timing Advantages

1. **Charter growth:** Sector adding ~100K students/year
2. **ESSER sunset:** Schools reviewing software spend, seeking ROI-focused solutions
3. **Analytics demand:** Data-driven instruction now standard expectation
4. **Consolidation opportunity:** Market fragmented, 12,300+ EdTech companies

### 6.2 Go-to-Market Priorities

1. **Target CMOs first:** 180 CMOs managing 23% of charter schools
2. **NAIS conference presence:** Direct access to 1,700 schools
3. **Seasonality:** Q3 (July-Sept) peak buying period
4. **Multi-year contracts:** Improve retention, predictable revenue

### 6.3 Valuation Implications

- EdTech SaaS/infrastructure: 18.6x EV/Revenue (2.4x market median)
- High NRR premium: 120% NRR = 2-3x higher valuation
- Buyers seek: Predictable revenue, high retention, sustainable margins
