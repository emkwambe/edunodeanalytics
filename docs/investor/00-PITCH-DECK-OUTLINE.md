## Presentation Tips

### For 10-Minute Pitch:
Focus on slides 1-10, 12-15

### For 30-Minute Meeting:
All slides, skip most appendix

### Key Messages to Emphasize:
1. **Problem is massive and painful** - $17.6B spent blind
2. **AI differentiation is real** - Not just marketing
3. **Go-to-market is smart** - Charter first, then scale
4. **Team can execute** - Right experience for this challenge
5. **Timing is perfect** - Learning loss + AI + ESSER

### Common Investor Questions:
1. "Why will districts buy from a startup?"
   → Charter references, ESA partnerships, superior AI

2. "How do you compete with Panorama's brand?"
   → We're intervention-focused, they're survey-focused

3. "What if incumbents add AI?"
   → 18-24 month head start, AI-first architecture

4. "How long is the sales cycle?"
   → 90 days for charters, 6-12 months for districts (hence charter-first)

5. "What's your defensibility?"
   → Proprietary metrics, AI models trained on outcomes data
