## Revenue Model

### Primary: Per-Student SaaS Subscription

| Pricing Tier | Price/Student/Year | Target Customer | Minimum ACV |
|--------------|-------------------|-----------------|-------------|
| **Starter** | $8-12 | Small schools (<500 students) | $4,000 |
| **Professional** | $12-18 | Mid-size districts (500-5,000) | $6,000 |
| **Enterprise** | $18-25 | Large districts (5,000+) | $90,000 |

### Why Per-Student Pricing?

1. **Predictable for Districts** - Easy to budget, scales with enrollment
2. **Aligned Incentives** - We succeed when we serve more students
3. **Competitive** - Industry standard, easy to compare
4. **Scalable** - Natural expansion as districts grow or add schools

## Pricing Strategy

### Tier Differentiation

#### Starter ($8-12/student)
**Included:**
- Dashboard Overview
- Basic MTSS tracking
- Student roster management
- Standard reporting
- Email support

**Target:** Small charter schools, private schools

#### Professional ($12-18/student)
**Everything in Starter, plus:**
- MTSS Intervention Hub (full)
- Student 360 Deep Dives
- AI Qualitative Pulse
- Attendance & behavior integration
- Priority support
- Admin training included

**Target:** Mid-size districts, charter networks

#### Enterprise ($18-25/student)
**Everything in Professional, plus:**
- AI Flight Plans
- Advanced Analytics
- Impact Analytics (ROI)
- Custom integrations
- White-label branding
- Dedicated CSM
- SLA guarantee
- API access

**Target:** Large districts, state deployments

### Pricing Philosophy

**Not the cheapest, not the most expensive.**

| Competitor | Price Range | Our Position |

| Branching Minds | $18-25 | Competitive on features, better AI |
| Panorama | $12-20 | More intervention-focused |
| Illuminate | $15-35 | Simpler, more accessible |
| EduNode | $12-25 | Best AI value |

## Unit Economics

### Target Metrics at Scale (Year 3+)

| Metric | Target | Industry Benchmark |
|--------|--------|-------------------|
| **Gross Margin** | 82% | 75-85% |
| **CAC (Blended)** | $18,000 | $15,000-25,000 |
| **CAC Payback** | 14 months | 12-24 months |
| **LTV** | $108,000 | $80,000-150,000 |
| **LTV:CAC** | 6:1 | 3:1 minimum |
| **Net Revenue Retention** | 115% | 100-120% |
| **Logo Churn** | <8% | 10-15% |

### Unit Economics by Segment

| Segment | Avg ACV | CAC | Payback | LTV | LTV:CAC |
|---------|---------|-----|---------|-----|---------|
| Small (<500) | $6,000 | $8,000 | 16 mo | $30,000 | 3.8:1 |

**Key Insight:** Mid-size and large districts have superior economics, justifying focused go-to-market.
