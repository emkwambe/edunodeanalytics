## The Opportunity

The U.S. K-12 education market spends **$17.6 billion annually on intervention programs**, yet **73% of districts report they cannot measure whether their interventions are actually working**. This represents a massive market inefficiency where billions are spent on interventions with no clear ROI tracking.

EduNode Analytics is a B2B SaaS platform that transforms how K-12 school districts track student growth, manage MTSS (Multi-Tiered System of Supports) interventions, and make data-driven decisions using AI-powered insights.

## The Problem

**1. The Data Fragmentation Crisis**
- Student data lives in 8-12+ disconnected systems (SIS, LMS, assessments, interventions)
- Teachers spend 5+ hours/week manually aggregating data
- 67% of intervention decisions are made on incomplete information

**2. The "Black Box" Problem**
- Districts invest heavily in Tier 2/3 interventions with no visibility into effectiveness
- When interventions fail, educators don't know if it's the program, dosage, or student fit
- $4,200 average cost per student for Tier 3 interventions with no outcome tracking

**3. The Compliance Burden**
- MTSS documentation requirements create 15+ hours of paperwork per student per year
- State reporting requirements increasing yearly
- FERPA compliance creates fear of innovation
